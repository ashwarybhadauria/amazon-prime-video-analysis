## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Amazon Prime Video's content catalog to identify trends in genres, content types, production countries, ratings, and content growth. It serves as a comprehensive exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the platform's library, offering actionable insights into content acquisition and platform trends.

## 📂 Dataset
The analysis is powered by two core datasets:
*   `titles.csv`: Contains metadata about movies and TV shows (titles, release year, genres, ratings, etc.)
*   `credits.csv`: Contains cast and crew information (actors, directors).

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
*   **Language:** Python
*   **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
*   **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
*   **Environment:** Google Colab

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas
*   **Distribution of Content:** Movies vs. TV Shows ratio.
*   **Top Genres:** Identifying the most prevalent content categories.
*   **Content Trends Over Time:** Analyzing platform growth and historical release years.
*   **Production Countries:** Geographic concentration of content creation.
*   **Ratings Analysis:** Comparing IMDb vs. TMDb Ratings.
*   **Key Contributors:** Actor and Director participation frequencies.
*   **Highly Acclaimed Content:** Identifying the highest-rated titles on the platform.

## 💡 Key Findings
*   **Format Dominance:** Movies account for approximately **86%** of the catalog.
*   **Top Genre:** **Drama** is the most common genre across the platform.
*   **Geographic Concentration:** The **United States** has the largest production representation.
*   **Growth Trend:** Content volume increased significantly **after 2010**.
*   **Top Rated:** *Pawankhind* had the highest IMDb rating among titles with at least 1,000 IMDb votes.

## 🚀 Conclusion
The analysis provides robust, data-driven insights that can help guide strategic decisions regarding content diversification, international representation, and future content investments.
